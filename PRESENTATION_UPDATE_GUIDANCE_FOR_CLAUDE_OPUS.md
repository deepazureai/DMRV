**3.2 Multi-Step Government Approval Workflow (Phase 2)**

Replace current "CCC Issuance" section with multi-step process:

- **Title**: "CCC Issuance: Government Governance Framework"
- **Current (Incorrect)**: "BEE Officer Reviews → Issues CCC"
- **NEW (Correct)**: 5-Step Process

```
CHECK-VERIFIED
    ↓
[1] BEE Bureau Prepares Dossier (1-2 weeks)
    - Compilation of submission documents
    - All verifier reports included
    - Administrative readiness check
    ↓
[2] NSCICM Expert Committee Review (2 weeks)
    - National Steering Committee (multi-expert board)
    - Methodology validation
    - Calculation accuracy verification
    ↓
[3] Central Government Approval (2 weeks)
    - Ministry-level final review
    - Policy compliance check
    - Formal approval decision
    ↓
[4] BEE Formally Issues CCC (Immediate)
    - Certificate generated
    - Blockchain registration
    - Entity notified
    ↓
CCC ISSUED (6-week total workflow)
```

- **Key Points to Emphasize**:
  - "We don't bypass government hierarchy. Approval flows through NSCICM → Central Govt → BEE."
  - "This embedding of government governance in the system is why we win trust."
  - "Each step has clear ownership and SLA (2 weeks NSCICM, 2 weeks Central Govt)."
  - "Status tracking allows government to monitor approval at each gate."

- **Visual**: Animated workflow showing:
  - Step-by-step progression with timelines
  - Status indicators (Pending → In Progress → Approved → Issued)
  - Government agency logos at each stage
  - Real-time status dashboard mockup

**3.3 Structured Verification Plan with Methodology (Phase 3)**

Add new section in ACVA workflow:

- **Title**: "ACVA Verification: Systematic Methodology Framework"
- **Content**: Before verification starts, ACVA prepares documented plan

**Verification Plan Components**:
1. **Risk Assessment**:
   - Data Quality Score: 87% (High confidence)
   - Risk Level: Low/Medium/High based on anomalies
   - Risk Factors: Complete data, <5% measurement uncertainty

2. **Sampling Strategy**:
   - Document Review: 25%
   - Record Sampling: 50%
   - On-site Assessment: 20%
   - Stakeholder Interviews: 10%

3. **14-Point Methodology Checklist** (BEE CCTS Standard):
   - Data Completeness
   - Data Quality & Measurement
   - Calculation Accuracy
   - Baseline Representativeness
   - Additionality Assessment
   - Methodology Compliance (ACM0013)
   - Leakage & Gaming Analysis
   - Documentation & Records
   - Monitoring Equipment & Calibration
   - Stakeholder Concerns
   - Regulatory Compliance
   - Special Provisions (renewable energy, CCUS, exported power)
   - Historical Consistency
   - Uncertainty Analysis

4. **Expected Timeline**:
   - Desk Review: 1-2 weeks
   - On-site Visit: 3-5 days
   - Report Preparation: 2-3 weeks
   - **Total**: 4-6 weeks

- **Key Points to Emphasize**:
  - "Verification isn't subjective spot-checking. It's systematic 14-point methodology."
  - "Every check is documented, referenced to BEE guidelines."
  - "This demonstrates we understand rigor, compliance, and reproducibility."
  - "Government leaders see we're not cutting corners—we're implementing best practice."

- **Visual**: Expandable verification plan card showing:
  - Risk level with color coding
  - Data quality progress bar
  - Checklist with expandable items
  - Timeline breakdown with weekly milestones
